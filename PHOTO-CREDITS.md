## Knife collection

All 16 knife images are photorealistic AI-generated studio illustrations created for CookPath using the built-in imagegen tool. They replace all earlier third-party knife photographs. They depict a fictional matching collection with nearly black ebony-style handles and satin steel blades, not actual products from a manufacturer. Prompts are saved in KNIFE-IMAGE-PROMPTS.md.

The Samin Nosrat publisher portrait remains a local review reference without an open reuse licence stated. Its publication status is unchanged.
